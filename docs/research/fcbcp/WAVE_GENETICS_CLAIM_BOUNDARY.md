## 0. Purpose

This document keeps FCBCP aligned with lawful physics and reproducible biology by separating:

- validated precursors;
- research hypotheses;
- metaphor-only language;
- excluded mechanisms.

The goal is not to suppress speculative history. The goal is to prevent unsupported wave-genetics claims from becoming solver assumptions, exportable HDT claims, research conclusions, or human-contact logic.
