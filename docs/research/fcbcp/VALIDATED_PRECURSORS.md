## 0. Purpose

This document captures lawful, evidence-backed precursor concepts that make FCBCP-style research scientifically discussable without importing unsupported wave-genetics claims.

The point is narrow: these precursors support field-biology research questions. They do not validate phantom-DNA fields, semantic wave-texts, DNA inductors, DNA speakers, holographic gene lasers, or human actuation.

## 1. Precursor P1 — field-matter coupling of biomolecules in controlled settings

Claim:

Biomolecules and biological nanoparticles can have field-addressable physical properties under controlled conditions. AC electrokinetic, dielectrophoretic, and impedance-based systems can manipulate or measure nucleic acids, extracellular vesicles, proteins, cells, and other particles in specific media and device geometries.

Allowed use:

- validated precursor discussion;
- instrumentation model;
- sample-preparation or sensor reasoning;
- evidence that field-biomolecule coupling can be lawful and measurable.

Boundary:

This does not imply semantic field genetics, phantom DNA, or in-vivo DNA acting as a circuit element. It also does not imply that exterior fields can safely or predictably control transcription in a human.

Evidence tier:

E5 within scoped laboratory / device contexts. Lower tier when generalized beyond those contexts.

## 2. Precursor P2 — 3D chromatin and regulatory architecture

Claim:

Genome function depends on more than one-dimensional sequence. Chromatin state, topology, accessibility, compartments, loops, and epigenetic marks affect transcriptional regulation.

Allowed use:

- biological mechanism spine;
- time-scale ladder;
- storage/code/computer metaphor when clearly bracketed;
- modeling of slow state variables such as chromatin accessibility proxies.

Boundary:

This does not imply holographic genome semantics, wave-copy organismal templates, or DNA reading language.

Evidence tier:

E5 within modern genomics and cell biology. Lower tier for any proposed field-driven modulation unless experimentally shown.

## 3. Precursor P3 — endogenous bioelectric signaling

Claim:

Cells and tissues use membrane potentials, ion channels, pumps, gap junctions, and bioelectric gradients in lawful physiological processes. Bioelectric state can interact with signaling pathways and gene expression.

Allowed use:

- state-space models where `Vm` or a membrane-polarization proxy precedes second messengers and transcriptional effects;
- research hypotheses around lagged cascades;
- distinction between endogenous and exogenous fields.

Boundary:

Endogenous bioelectricity does not by itself validate safe exterior control, transcript steering, or human actuation. Any exogenous route must pass transfer, dose, mechanism, and safety gates.

Evidence tier:

E5 for endogenous bioelectric physiology within declared scope. Lower tier for FCBCP external modulation until tested.

## 4. Precursor P4 — lawful modality kernels

Claim:

Light, heat, sound, electric fields, and magnetic-particle systems can couple to biological systems through declared physical mechanisms: absorption, heating, pressure, membrane polarization, mechanotransduction, particle-mediated effects, or construct-specific pathways.

Allowed use:

- modality-kernel table in FCBCP;
- simulation and phantom fixtures;
- ex vivo research designs;
- safety-envelope formulation.

Boundary:

Each kernel must be declared and separately validated. A modality name is not a mechanism. A successful optical, acoustic, or magnetic effect in one setting does not generalize to human-boundary use.

Evidence tier:

E1-E5 depending on the kernel and evidence. Human-contact schedule safety remains blocked by default.

## 5. Precursor P5 — measurable lag cascade

Claim:

Fast physical effects and slower biological responses generally occupy different time scales. A field-like perturbation may produce immediate physical or membrane-level effects, while transcript, protein, chromatin, and tissue changes occur later if they occur at all.

Allowed use:

- falsifiable hypothesis design;
- FCBCP cascade-lag tests;
- time-scale ladder;
- distinguishing artifact from plausible mechanism.

Boundary:

Instant transcript changes without plausible intermediate measurements should be treated as suspicious or artifact-prone. Absence of a lagged response can falsify a proposed pathway.

Evidence tier:

E1 as modeling discipline; higher only in endpoint-specific experiments.

## 6. Single-cell versus multicellular scope

Unicellular systems:

- membrane potential and ion gradients can rapidly affect physiology;
- nucleoid/chromatin analogues differ by organism;
- field coupling may be local and direct in controlled experiments;
- released or extracellular nucleic acids may be electrically addressable in device contexts.

Multicellular systems:

- tissues add layered boundaries, extracellular matrix, vascular/perfusion effects, gap junctions, immune/endocrine state, and heterogeneous microenvironments;
- effective field coupling is filtered by surface, tissue, geometry, and safety constraints;
- human-scale inference requires consent, minimization, and protection gates.

## 7. What these precursors do not validate

They do not validate:

- phantom-DNA fields;
- persistent post-DNA-removal wave imprints;
- DNA reading semantic language;
- DNA, RNA, or nucleosomes as in-vivo lumped inductors/speakers/microphones/lasers;
- biological magnets native to DNA;
- mechanism inferred from shape resemblance;
- human-safe field control of transcription;
- autonomous human actuation.
